The Risk of the Tears To Steven, and to the timeless worlds of The Little Prince that we once shared.

I met him in the winter.

He was as ardent and warm as the sun,
as free and untamed as the breeze.

Chapter Two

Long Long Journey

We walked a long way side by side -- amidst the heavy snows of the Blue Mountains, and through the hot air in plane-tree-shaded streets of Shanghai.

Together, we have watched the sun rise at 5 A.M., and have listened to the bells toll at midnight.

To be with him was to be truly happy.

Chapter Three

The Friend

Then, one day, he said to me: 'Let's be friends.'

I thought for a moment, and then said to him with all my heart: 'Everyone who has ever been a friend to me,
I have somehow lost along the way.'

Chapter Four

I See You

He looked at me in silence.

I tried to comfort him: 'I love the way you look at me. Your eyes make me feel that I am truly here.'

'That is because I love you,' he said.

Chapter Five

Almost Friend

And so we went on walking together--


He would bring me breakfast on the subway.

He would hide a bouquet of pink roses within his backpack
and give it to me in surprise.

We crowded together in my tiny dorm room, watching k-drama.

We found a rooftop, gazing at the sunset after a long, tiring day.




In every instant he looked at me,
I felt as though we were friends.

Chapter Six

Tears

As the end drew near,
I felt a deep, echoing regret that I had not embraced him,
that I had not told him how much, how very much,
I longed to be his friend.

I suddenly remembered the words from The Little Prince
that we both loved:

'One runs the risk of crying a bit if one allows oneself to be tamed.'

Chapter Seven

To My Rose

He said we weren't afraid of tears, so long as it was worth it.


I was never afraid because--
every tear is worth it.
